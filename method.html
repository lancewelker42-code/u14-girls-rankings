Our national rank (the # column)

  • A rating for every team from goal differences in all games we collect (capped at ±5 goals), with league and conference strength terms: rating = league + conference + team, fitted with ridge shrinkage so thin data pulls a team toward its league's average.
  • League and conference strengths come only from games between leagues and conferences. Ratings are never hand-edited.
  • A team needs at least 5 games to be ranked. The top 50 is simply our national ranks 1–50.
  • Season window: games from June 1, 2026 (age groups roll over June 1). Girls 2012/13 only; boys, other birth years and cross-age games are excluded by team name.
  • When two records on a platform are clearly the same team, their games are combined (games listed under both are counted once).

Composite Rank (reference)

  • Blends up to six inputs, each mapped to 0–100: our rating rank (weight .30), PitchRank (.25), USA Rank (.15), GotSport (.10), Table & Pitch league place (.10, tracked conferences only) and a schedule-adjusted record (.10). Weights are renormalized over the inputs a team has; at least 3 inputs are required.
  • Published ranks are converted with 100·(1 − ln r ⁄ ln N). Third-party rank numbers are used only as inputs and are not reproduced on this site.

Ground rules

  • Scores, ranks and records are never invented. "—" means the value is not available or a name match was not certain.
  • A league place with 0 games played is only a listing order and is never treated as a ranking.
  • Game data accumulates week to week (games are added, never deleted), so accuracy improves through the season.
  • City/State is taken from GotSport team records or the club's own website or league page; it is never guessed. State alone means no city was found.

Known limitations

  • ECNL and ECNL RL league and showcase games are not on GotSport; since November 17, 2026 we read them for every conference from the ECNL's own results system. Some tournaments credit teams on GotSport but publish no scores we can read (some require a login), so a few teams still have fewer games here than they played.
  • Teams with 5–6 games can move a lot from week to week.
  • Team names are matched across sources conservatively; an unmatched team simply has fewer Composite inputs.

The map

The map above the table places a dot at each top-50 team’s city. Top-10 teams use a gold dot, and the same gold is used for their number in the # column. Coordinates come from the U.S. Census Bureau Gazetteer (internal point of the place or, if the city is a town that is not a Census place, the county subdivision) or, for a few unincorporated communities, the USGS Geographic Names Information System. State outlines are the Census cartographic boundary file (1:20,000,000). A team with no confirmed city is omitted and counted in the caption. Coordinates are never guessed.

Data sources

  • GotSport rankings: national team rankings and match results (game data source)
  • PitchRank: national U14 girls rankings
  • USA Rank: national youth soccer rankings
  • Table & Pitch: ECNL / ECNL RL league standings and results
  • Surf Cup results: Surf Cup (San Diego) girls U14 tournament results, July 31 - Aug 3, 2026 (game data source)
  • ECNL results (Total Global Sports): ECNL and ECNL Regional League girls U14 league, League Cup and national showcase results, from the league's own results system (game data source)
  • Athletes2Events tournament results: public results of summer and Labor Day tournaments (Surf Cup NW, Manchester City Cup, Girlazo, Zipfizz Cup, ZF Labor Day Challenge, Nomads/STS events, Mustang Stampede) (game data source)
