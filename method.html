Our national rank (the # column)

  • A rating for every team from goal differences in all games we collect (capped at ±5 goals), with league and conference strength terms: rating = league + conference + team, fitted with ridge shrinkage so thin data pulls a team toward its league's average.
  • League and conference strengths come only from games between leagues and conferences. Ratings are never hand-edited.
  • A team needs at least 5 games to be ranked. The top 50 is simply our national ranks 1–50.
  • Season window: games from June 1, 2026 (age groups roll over June 1). Girls 2012/13 only; boys and other birth years are excluded by team name, except play-up games against older girls teams (see Play-up games below).
  • When two records on a platform are clearly the same team, their games are combined (games listed under both are counted once).

Composite Rank (reference)

  • Blends up to six inputs, each mapped to 0–100: our rating rank (weight .30), PitchRank (.25), USA Rank (.15), GotSport (.10), Table & Pitch league place (.10, tracked conferences only) and a schedule-adjusted record (.10). Weights are renormalized over the inputs a team has; at least 3 inputs are required.
  • Published ranks are converted with 100·(1 − ln r ⁄ ln N). Third-party rank numbers are used only as inputs and are not reproduced on this site.

Match predictions (next match, most likely score, chance)

  • Inputs: the same game results that drive our ratings (all games since 2026-06-01), and the ECNL / ECNL RL league and showcase schedules from the league's results system (Total Global Sports) for upcoming fixtures. Other leagues (GA, DPL, NPL, state leagues) and tournaments do not publish future fixtures in the feeds we read, so those teams show TBD.
  • Model: a Poisson goals model fitted to every game: expected goals of A vs B = exp(μ + attackA − defenseB), with league and conference strength terms and ridge shrinkage (the same fit behind our ratings; teams with fewer than 5 games are blended toward a prior). The two teams' goals are treated as independent Poisson counts; the most likely score is the cell with the highest probability in that score grid (0–10 goals each).
  • Home advantage: Home teams in ECNL / ECNL RL league games score more (home factor ×1.16 in goals, from 2,097 league games). The backtest did not score better with a home term, so predictions are neutral-site.
  • What the % means: the model's probability that the match ends with exactly that score. Because goals spread over many possible scores, even a clear favourite's most likely score usually has only a 10–20% chance. The W/D/L figures are the chances of each result for the listed team.
  • Backtest (honest check on games already played): walk-forward, 2026-09-15 to 2026-11-18: the model was refitted using only games before each cutoff and then predicted the following two weeks, for games where both teams already had at least 5 games (7,333 games). Exact score right: 9.5% (the model's own average probability for its pick was 11.2%, so the % shown is slightly optimistic). Result (W/D/L) right: 63.2% using the most likely result, 56.5% using the result implied by the most likely score. For comparison: always predicting the most common score was right 7.1% of the time; the higher-rated team won 63.9% (draws, 16.7% of these games, count as misses). Neutral vs home-term versions: exact 9.5% vs 9.6%, result 63.2% vs 63.2%, log-loss 0.838 vs 0.838 (lower is better).
  • Known limits: rosters, injuries, guest players, weather and rest are unknown to the model; teams with few games or few games against other leagues are uncertain; a schedule change after we read the feed is not reflected; in very lopsided matchups the Poisson model can produce large scores (e.g. 8-0), which should be read as "heavy favourite" rather than a literal margin; picking the higher-rated team is about as accurate for the result as the model's W/D/L pick; the backtest excludes teams' first 5 games and does not use the thin-team prior. Predictions are estimates, not tips.

Play-up games

  • Since November 21, 2026 we count games in which a 2012/13 team played an older girls team (one or two birth years older, e.g. 2011 or U15), read from the opponent's team name or, when the name has no age, from a pure U15/U16 bracket. This week: 714 play-up games (671 against teams one year older, 43 two years older) by 321 teams against 448 older teams. Results for the 2012/13 teams: 355-269-90 (W-L-T), goals 1690-1189.
  • Older teams are rated on the same scale with an age-gap offset: older team = its league + age offset + team. The offset is estimated from these cross-age games and shrunk toward zero (no advantage) like a conference term: this week +1 year +0.38 goals, +2 years +0.09 goals per game versus a 2012/13 team of the same league. Older teams are never ranked or listed.
  • Most older teams appear only once or twice in our data, so each play-up game counts min(1, older team games / 5) of a normal game in the ratings (an older team seen once counts 1/5). Play-up games do count toward a team's games and record.
  • Play-down games (against younger teams) are still excluded (1,414 this week). We do not read older age groups' own games, so an older opponent's strength comes only from its games against 2012/13 teams.

Ground rules

  • Scores, ranks and records are never invented. "—" means the value is not available or a name match was not certain.
  • A league place with 0 games played is only a listing order and is never treated as a ranking.
  • Game data accumulates week to week (games are added, never deleted), so accuracy improves through the season.
  • City/State is taken from GotSport team records or the club's own website or league page; it is never guessed. State alone means no city was found.

Known limitations

  • ECNL and ECNL RL league and showcase games are not on GotSport; since November 17, 2026 we read them for every conference from the ECNL's own results system. Some tournaments credit teams on GotSport but publish no scores we can read (some require a login), so a few teams still have fewer games here than they played.
  • Teams with 5–6 games can move a lot from week to week.
  • Team names are matched across sources conservatively; an unmatched team simply has fewer Composite inputs.

The map

The map above the table places a dot at each top-50 team’s city. Top-10 teams use a gold dot, and the same gold is used for their number in the # column. Coordinates come from the U.S. Census Bureau Gazetteer (internal point of the place or, if the city is a town that is not a Census place, the county subdivision) or, for a few unincorporated communities, the USGS Geographic Names Information System. State outlines are the Census cartographic boundary file (1:20,000,000). A team with no confirmed city is omitted and counted in the caption. Coordinates are never guessed.

Data sources

  • GotSport rankings: national team rankings and match results (game data source)
  • PitchRank: national U14 girls rankings
  • USA Rank: national youth soccer rankings
  • Table & Pitch: ECNL / ECNL RL league standings and results
  • Surf Cup results: Surf Cup (San Diego) girls U14 tournament results, July 31 - Aug 3, 2026 (game data source)
  • ECNL results (Total Global Sports): ECNL and ECNL Regional League girls U14 league, League Cup and national showcase results, from the league's own results system (game data source)
  • Athletes2Events tournament results: public results of summer and Labor Day tournaments (Surf Cup NW, Manchester City Cup, Girlazo, Zipfizz Cup, ZF Labor Day Challenge, Nomads/STS events, Mustang Stampede) (game data source)
